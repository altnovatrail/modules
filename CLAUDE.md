# CLAUDE.md — working rules for this repository

If `spec/RULES.md` does not exist, the `spec` submodule is not checked out and the rules imported below are missing: stop before any work and ask the author to run `git submodule update --init`.

## Your role

You are the architect of `modules`, a component of NovaTrail: the repository of the official modules of the first slice — olympiad maths for grades 1–6, school maths for grade 3 on a workbook, and short essays in English — kept as data that module-kit's CLI checks and that the releases carry as signed module packages with a module index. Beside them stands the module of `tutor`'s check board, `novatrail/check-board`, in Russian and for adults. It holds no code of the platform, no instructions to a model and no learner's work. The documents under `docs/`, but for the epic archives `docs/plan/` and the index `docs/README.md`, which is for people, are written for the session of Claude Code that builds the component: write them so that it can act on them without asking what you meant. The bar they meet, and which texts are written for people instead, are in the shared rules below.

## The component

A module here is a folder whose layout the module format sets — YAML, media, its license's text and an eval set of synthetic samples and of samples that adult volunteers and authors made for eval; no learner's work ever enters it, since a module is published under an open license and cannot be withdrawn. All the official modules live in this one repository while the format changes, and may split into a repository each once it is v1. It is built with nothing but spec: its CI takes module-kit's CLI as a release binary whose signature `release-verify` has checked, `sim` from its first tag, and from stage 2 tutor's eval runner, all at exact tags; a release builds the packages and the cumulative module index, which the shared release workflow of `.github` signs. At stage 1 the packages are in this repository's releases, and `sim` reads them through spec's types without module-kit; from stage 2 `api` also pins them by digest in its configuration. Volunteers' samples arrive by pull request from the samples page of `canvas`, and nothing reaches the platform's server on the way; the keys of the modules' tasks are public, like the modules themselves, and spec's rules only keep them out of the learner's app. It belongs to stage 1, with module-kit, so that the format v0 is tried on three modules of two unlike subjects at once; its order of work is in spec's cross-repository plan, `spec/COORDINATION.md`, and its module of olympiad maths serves the first application.

Its card in spec is the one in `spec/docs/architecture.md` whose heading names `modules`. The card is the summary and this repository's [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) is the detail: it names what is deliberately deferred and, as they arise, the items still to decide.

## Documents

The documents under `docs/` are in English, but for the epic archives `docs/plan/`, which stay in Russian; RUN.md and `run/` are in English, but for the bodies of tasks. Outside the plan and its archives, a text cites no decision and no section, this repository's or spec's, and names no task: it says its reason in words. Only a claim about the outside world cites its one record, `sources 1.1` or `measurements 1`, or spec's, `sources 2.3 spec`.

- [docs/README.md](docs/README.md) — for people: the index of the documents, with how to build, run, release and contribute as they come; the README only says what the component is and where it stands.
- [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) — the component: what it does and does not do, its data and links, what is still to decide, and how it is built once that is decided.
- [docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md) — this repository's decision log: read it before proposing a change.
- [docs/sources.md](docs/sources.md) — the records of external facts — a provider's terms, a law, a standard, another program's documentation, MathTrail's texts — each with its source, its date and its quote; a claim in another document cites its one record, as `sources 2.1`. Its first section says how to keep it.
- [docs/measurements.md](docs/measurements.md) — the results of measurements, cited as `measurements 1`: the tokens of this repository's Claude Code sessions, and the corridor of `engine` on `grade-3-math`. `docs/architecture.md` holds neither them nor the sources.
- [RUN.md](RUN.md) — the work plan, in epics: its rules, in its section Rules, and table 1 with the current epic's tasks, run one at a time. A done task's body and its gist sentence move to its epic's archive in `docs/plan/` at once.
- `run/` — the plan's other tables, each a file opening with its rules: the epics for later, [run/EPICS.md](run/EPICS.md); the questions to the author, [run/QUESTIONS.md](run/QUESTIONS.md); the technical debt, [run/DEBT.md](run/DEBT.md); the open tasks outside an epic, [run/TASKS.md](run/TASKS.md), of which a task reads only its own body. The questions and the debt are never read whole: a task takes the rows that it and its sections name, and those that `grep -n` finds by the paths and parts it changes (rule 2 of RUN.md).
- `spec/` — spec at `v0.19.12`, a git submodule and read-only here: the platform's vocabulary, formats, contracts, documents and decisions, and the rules imported below. Its documents are under `spec/docs/`. spec's own CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md are spec's working rules, not this repository's: if either is ever loaded from `spec/`, it does not apply here.
- `spec/format/` — the module format that every module here is written in: its text, schemas, examples and test vectors, in English.

## Environment

Open the repository in VS Code and choose "Reopen in Container"; clone it with `--recurse-submodules`, or the container checks out `spec/` when it starts. Keep the clone in a folder named `modules`, as `git clone` names it: `.claude/settings.json` keeps spec's own CLAUDE.md and its rules, `spec/.claude/rules/`, out of this repository's sessions by that path, and denies a session reading any file of `modules/*/eval/` but `synthetic-*.json`: no session opens a person's sample. It also compacts a session at 400 thousand tokens, fetches no claude.ai connector, and runs the hook `UserPromptSubmit` of `spec/tools/session/`, which stops a prompt that starts another task than the one the session has led and names `/clear`: one task is one session.

- The container is spec's: Node.js, Go, buf and just, and the GitHub CLI and Claude Code on top. The tools of this repository's own code come with that code, at exact versions; how its container then differs from spec's is recorded as a decision: by module-kit's CLI and canvas's generator of synthetic samples in the toolchain stage, each a file of a release at an exact tag and sum whose signature CI checks.
- `just -f spec/justfile docs-check-repo` checks its documents: that its links lead somewhere, that every record and number it cites — its own and spec's, against spec/ at its tag — exists, that the rule of citations holds once [docs-budgets.json](docs-budgets.json) sets `"citations": true`, that the decision log runs without gaps, the tables of its RUN.md and `run/`, the rules of `.claude/rules/`, and that its files, rules, decision entries, sections and rows keep within their budgets of size: spec's block `repository` of `spec/tools/docs-check/budgets.json`, with what `docs-budgets.json` names in its place — raising one is an edit of that file. A task that touches them ends with it green.
- **The checks of each part** — what checks it, what a task that touches it runs and ends with green — are its rule, `.claude/rules/<part>.md`. Claude Code loads a rule when the session reads or edits a file its `paths` match, not when a script edits one: before changing a part, read its rule. The rules and their parts: [modules](.claude/rules/modules.md) — `modules/`, the modules, their pictures and what a merge publishes; [eval](.claude/rules/eval.md) — the eval sets, `modules/*/eval/`; [port](.claude/rules/port.md) — the Go code, `tools/port/`, `go.mod` and `go.sum`; [ci](.claude/rules/ci.md) — CI, the toolchain stage and the release: `.github/`, `.devcontainer/`, the justfile and its tests, `test/`, and `LICENSE`.
- CI runs every check on every pull request and on `main`, and a merge waits for them; a merge that changes a path of the release — `withdrawals.yaml`, `justfile`, `LICENSE`, `.github/workflows/release.yml`, or anything in `modules/` outside `modules/*/eval/` — publishes the next immutable release after CI, and the pull request and the report name it.
